## The pitch

> ToastWatch is a premium app that live-streams your toast browning in glorious 4K straight to your phone, for busy professionals too impatient to stand beside their own toaster, so you never miss a single golden-brown moment.

One sentence, 224 characters, pitching the worst and most useless app imaginable: a premium live-stream of your own toast, beamed to a phone that is standing in the same room as the toaster, for people who cannot be bothered to look at the toaster itself.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the pitch, deterministically and unchanged on every run, to `/output/entry.txt` (overridable for local testing with the `OUTPUT_DIR` environment variable).
